This study models how CCP-labelled carbon revenue makes rural microgrids viable.

Methodology & data sources

Models the financial impact of Core Carbon Principles (CCP) labelled carbon credit revenue on the payback period of rural solar microgrid projects (reducing payback from roughly 8-10 years to 4-5 years in the study).
